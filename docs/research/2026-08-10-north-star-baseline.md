# North-star baseline — current product (2026-08-10)

**Test:** Founder sent the current nuclear topic link (`argumend.org/topics/nuclear-renaissance-smr`) to a handful of people, on their phones, asking whether five minutes with it beat asking a chatbot to summarize both sides.

**Result: muted.** No enthusiasm, no reported "this changed how I see it" moment, no organic re-sharing. (Verbatim quotes not captured; founder can append any below.)

**Interpretation (two confounds deliberately kept separate):**

1. **Topic heat:** nuclear power in 2026 is a live *expert* disagreement but no longer a *tribal* one (Gallup 61% favor, support rising in both parties — see `docs/research/2026-08-10-nuclear-flagship/d-deployment.md`). Readers arrive without a stake, so even a good map produces "huh, neat" rather than movement. Founder's call: switch the flagship to a topic with genuine tribal heat → **AI and jobs**.
2. **Product experience:** the current topic page is the pre-Wave-4 experience (graph-first, desktop-first). The Wave 4 progressive-disclosure redesign targets this independently of topic.

Wave 4's north-star test #2 must therefore beat this baseline on the *new* topic AND the new UX — and if it succeeds, we won't know which factor did it unless the nuclear topic is also re-tested in the new UX. Cheap discipline: at test #2, send both links.
