# Round 2: accessibility and dark mode, measured and fixed (2026-09-29)

Branch `r2/a11y-dark`, off `ux/round2-2026-09-29` (17a28db). Seven fix and test commits, then this report.

## What was measured

- **axe-core 4** (wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21a, wcag21aa, best-practice), injected with Playwright into
  the dev server. 32 routes: the ones the brief lists, plus `/analyze` in its result state (the
  example pasted and submitted). Each route ran at 390 and 1440 wide, in light and in dark (theme
  set through next-themes' `localStorage.theme`, and the `.dark` class checked on every run).
  That is 128 runs before and 128 after. Pages were scrolled end to end before the run so that any
  scroll-triggered content was on screen.
- **Focus indicators.** On 13 key pages, both themes and both widths, Tab was pressed until focus
  cycled back to the first element (2,520 stops). At each stop the script took the focused
  element's outline or ring, composited it over the surface behind the element, and flagged it
  when the contrast was under 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11 non-text contrast, which covers focus indicators).
- **Keyboard and screen-reader flows** (Playwright, both themes): the skip link, the phone menu
  (open, focus trap, Escape, focus return), search (⌘K, arrow keys, Enter, Escape), crux folds,
  reflection chips, Save/Share/Embed, the paste flow (type, submit, the result, "Open the map at
  this crux"), and the glossary A–Z bar. axe also ran on each open state (menu sheet, search
  dialog, embed dialog, paste result). A MutationObserver recorded what every live region said,
  with `aria-hidden` text left out.
- **Dark visual pass.** For every route: screenshots in dark at 390 and 1440, of the first screen
  and of a screen two viewport heights down (256 images), reviewed as contact sheets.
- **Reduced motion.** `/perspectives` had each scene scrolled into view, with transforms sampled
  mid-entrance and axe run on the scene once it had entered. On 17 routes, the script listed
  animations still running 1.5 s after load.

Raw results (JSON per run), screenshots, contact sheets and the scripts (`audit.mjs`, `focus.mjs`,
`flows.mjs`, `persp.mjs`, `motion.mjs`, `summarize.mjs`) are in
`/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-amirjalali-argumend/2043480f-f401-456b-87a7-9f7cc9a30f41/scratchpad/round2/a11y/`
(`before/`, `after/`, `focus-*.json`, `flows-*.json`). axe was installed there; it is not a
dependency of the repo.

### Dark visual pass

The pages hold up well in dark mode: surfaces, text tokens, chips, the rust CTA (white on
rust-600, 5.0:1, the same in both themes) and borders all read correctly. What I found:

- The parchment illustrations (blog and guide heroes, blog thumbnails, the perspectives scenes)
  glared at full brightness on #1a1917.
- React Flow's attribution badge kept its half-white box in the corner of the dark diagram.
- The search dialog's active result was marked by a 20% teal ring, invisible in dark.
- The citation index badge (a numbered disc) was stone-100 in both themes, so a pale disc on
  dark. It was also 4.35:1 for 10px text in light.
- The earlier "header floats 20px down" look in scrolled screenshots was a capture artifact of
  smooth scrolling. The header sits at top: 0 (checked).

### Reduced motion

`ThemeProvider`'s `<MotionConfig reducedMotion="user">` and the global `prefers-reduced-motion`
block do their job. On `/perspectives`, with the preference set, no scene element carries a
transform while it enters (49 do without it). The scenes still fade in opacity, which the
preference allows. axe is clean on every scene, in both themes, once it has entered. No route has
an animation still running at rest. The paste flow's scroll-to-result already checks the
preference.

## Fixes (commits on `r2/a11y-dark`)

1. **`ad0ae9e` One theme-aware focus ring token.** Adds `--focus-ring-rgb` to `globals.css`
   (#3a6965 light, 5.5:1 on the canvas; #7fb5b0 dark, 7.6:1) and the Tailwind colour `focus`. A
   scripted rename, limited to the ring-colour classes, points every focus ring at it
   (`focus-visible:ring-deep/40|/50|/60`, bare `ring-deep`, `ring-rust-500`, `ring-rust-600/60`,
   `ring-[#4f7b77]/50`, the inputs' `focus:ring-deep/20|/30` and `focus:ring-rust-500/30`,
   `has-[:focus-visible]:ring-deep/40`). The ten `dark:focus-visible:ring-[#6fa39e]` overrides
   are deleted. The global `:focus-visible` outline uses the same variable, which removes the
   `.dark :focus-visible` override. The footer signup heading becomes an h2.
2. **`79c53d4` Contrast.** Paste result and map-reply side labels (`MapResult`, `TurnList`,
   `EvidenceCards`, `meters`) now use `toneStyles.rust|brown.accentText`, the tone map's AA pairs.
   The privacy and terms footnote drops `dark:text-stone-500`. Collection chip counts are full
   colour at a lighter weight. `aria-current` moves onto the chip link through a new optional
   `current` prop on `Chip`, and a space keeps the link's name "Science 31". The search dialog in
   dark gets dark shades for the Esc button, the active title, its icons and the search glyph, and
   the Esc button's name becomes "Close search (Esc)", which includes its visible label.
3. **`2bf4675` Announcements and focus.** `/analyze` gets a persistent status region, and its
   countdown is `aria-hidden`. The crux reflection gets a persistent status region. The
   `/questions` search keeps its live region in the page at all times, and `aria-controls` only
   points at the results list while that list exists. The Share panel's Escape returns focus to
   Share.
4. **`5944991` Dark CSS.** `.dark main img:not([data-no-dim]) { filter: brightness(0.86) }`. The
   React Flow attribution badge takes the canvas and muted-text tokens in dark.
5. **`77daed0` + `7dbed91` Guards and the last rings.** `lib/focusRingTokens.test.ts` computes the
   ring's contrast from `globals.css` against the canvas, card and panel surfaces, in both themes
   (≥ 3:1). It fails on any focus ring in another colour, on any dark-only hex ring, and on any
   ring width left without a colour, and it checks every Button variant. The last check caught
   the diagram's "Find the crux" button, which drew Tailwind's default faded blue; it is fixed.
   The search dialog's active result now uses the focus ring. Component tests cover the
   reflection's live region, the paste announcement and the once-only hint, Share's Escape focus
   return, and the `/questions` live region and `aria-controls`.
6. **`3d01e02` Citation badge.** `bg-subtle text-muted`, and the teal hover gets its dark shade.

## Left, and why

- **Dark rust text (founder decision 14 in `docs/plans/2026-09-22-ledger-v1-run.md`).** The
  `--rust-text` token still maps to rust-500 in dark, 3.87 to 4.29:1. I did not change the token.
  The two surfaces axe caught moved to the tone map's existing small-text pair (rust-300 in dark),
  which was already used by `toneStyles.rust.accentText` and the rust chips. `TopicPage` and
  `MapResult`'s crux labels use rust-400 (#d4805f) in dark and pass. The token itself is still
  your call.
- **Placeholders.** Several inputs use `placeholder-stone-400` or `placeholder:text-muted/80` (2.5
  to 3.5:1). axe does not test placeholder text, and every field has a real label, so I left it.
- **The white skip link in dark.** A white pill with dark text and a teal outline. It is visible
  and intentional, so I kept it.
- **Arriving from "Open the map at this crux".** The crux heading lands at 72 px, just below the
  header, but its fold stays closed. This is not an accessibility failure. Whether the fold should
  open on arrival is a question for the topic template's owner.
- **`/perspectives` scenes** fade out again when scrolled away (`once: false`). That is allowed
  under reduced motion (opacity only), and assistive tech still reads the text, which sits in the
  DOM at opacity 0. I left the design as it is.
- **Not audited:** `/reply`, `/analyze-v2` (it redirects), `?view=read`, and the worksheet pages.
  The map-reply side labels are fixed by the same change as the paste result, but I did not run
  axe on `/reply`.
