- **central-bank-digital-currency 2: the evidence sides look inverted.** Both cards (M-Pesa
  reaching 96% without a CBDC, and the eNaira's low adoption) back the supporters' text
  ("a solution in search of a problem"). Both are marked `against`. Evidence cards were
  outside this pass, and evidence `side` feeds map-reply's "strongest weighted evidence per
  side", so they are unchanged.
- **section-230-reform 1 skeptic flip.** It is the right way round, but its wording is
  "…is no longer the settled judicial or empirical view". The flip predates this pass, so it
  is unchanged. It could be reworded if "settled" should stay off the page.
- **open-weight-ai-models 2 supporter flip.** After the swap it cites the LLaMA leak and the
  NTIA non-recall finding. Both sides already accept those facts (they are listed as common
  ground), so it is a weak flip, though it no longer points the wrong way. A stronger one
  would need a fact that is not in the map yet.
- **Prose order.** Some swapped texts were written as a reply, and they still read as one on
  their new side. Examples: the term-limits 3 supporters' text opens "Even granting the legal
  hurdle…", and the deepfakes 3 skeptics' text opens "The transition challenge is real but…".
  The meaning is on the right side; the rhetorical order is not (the audit reported the same
  for its swaps).
