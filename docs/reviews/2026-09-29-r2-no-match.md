## 1. Weak overlap is hidden

Before this change, a paste with no map named got up to three "closest maps" when:

- the best map scored at least 8, or
- the best map covered 30% of a short paste's words.

A long text clears a raw score of 8 easily, so most negatives got a list anyway.

The new rule is in `decideMatch` in `lib/paste/maps.ts`. With no map named, closest maps are
listed only when all three of these hold:

- **No map stood clear.** One map standing clear of other subjects with too little text behind it
  to be named means the paste is borrowing that map's words. Examples: a landlord who won't fix the
  boiler and the rent-control map; school uniforms and phone bans. Listing that map would name it
  by the back door.
- **The best map scores at least 8.** This was already the floor.
- **The best map covers at least 20% of the paste's words.** This is new. It removes the long
  match reports, recipe blogs and product reviews.

Measured on the eval set (`data/evals/paste-matching`: 111 dev + 42 holdout, 37 negatives) plus
the three assisted-living pastes I wrote:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| negatives shown a closest-maps list | 10 of 40 (9 of the eval's 37, plus 1 of mine) | **0** |
| negatives named as a map | 1 (`neg-abortion`, see below) | 1 (unchanged) |
| right map named (top-1) | unchanged | unchanged |
| wrong map named | unchanged | unchanged |
| right map among those shown (top-3) | 112/116 = 96.6% | 109/116 = **94.0%** (floor 93%) |

The top-3 drop comes from three positives whose "closest" list no longer appears:

- `billionaires-policy-failure`: score 6.3
- `chatgpt-water`: covers 17% of its words
- `hold-berlin-rent-freeze`: the rent-control map stood clear but had too little text behind it

The Berlin one is a real loss. It is the same pattern as the landlord negative, and the
aggregate scores cannot tell the two apart. Any matched answer, and the maps listed beside it, is
unchanged.

The assisted-living conversation (`lib/paste/testPastes.ts`) used to list loneliness, AI mass
unemployment and return-to-office. It now lists nothing. The live-site nuclear paste still names
`nuclear-energy-safety`.

**Left alone, flagged:** `neg-abortion` is named as `artificial-reproduction-ethics`, with
assisted dying shown as closely related. The r2 report measured 0 of 25 dev negatives named, so
this regressed after r2, most likely through the round-3 map text. The eval floor allows one false
positive, so CI stays green. Fixing it means changing the naming rule, which is outside this task.

## 2. "Read it yourself" guide

The guide is `components/paste/ReadItYourself.tsx`. It shows whenever no map is named, unless a
diagnosis of the text is already on the page. It is deterministic, offline and uses no model. It
asks three questions:

1. **Is this about a fact, a value, or a word?**
   - Fact and value use the site's own definitions (`questionKinds` in `lib/questionMeta.ts`, the
     same text as the `/questions` legend), labelled "empirical" and "normative".
   - Word ("definitional") echoes `lib/disagreement/labels.ts`.
   - Each has one everyday example.
   - Below them sits **"Simple cues from your words"**, from `lib/paste/cues.ts`. It is a pure word
     search that quotes the reader's own sentence with the most cue words of each kind:
     - value: should, ought, fair, deserve, responsible and similar
     - fact: numbers, cost, studies, data, more than and similar
     - word: "counts as", "definition", a word in quotation marks
   - The cues are labelled "A word search, not a reading. It can be wrong." For the assisted-living
     paste they quote "Aides cost more than the facility…" (something to check) and "Assisted living
     is the responsible choice." (a value word).
2. **What would change each person's mind?** Two fill-in lines: "You would think again if…" and
   "They would think again if…".
3. **What do you both already agree on?** One fill-in line.

The fill-in lines are uncontrolled inputs. Nothing reads them back, and they are not sent, stored
or put in the summary. The page says so under them. A test types into them and asserts that no
request goes out and nothing is stored.

After the three questions:

- a closing line: "Where the answers meet is the crux…"
- links to "What a crux is" (`/concepts/cruxes`) and "Browse all maps" (`/topics`).

## 3. What's possible with the diagnosis lane off

When the lane is off, the page shows one line: "A fuller reading of an argument that is not on a
map, one that lays out each side and finds its crux, needs Argumend's AI diagnosis, which is not
switched on here." The heading is now always "No map, rather than the wrong map". The "none" lede
reads "None of Argumend's 159 maps is about this, so none is named."

"How this was read" gains one sentence on when closest maps are listed.

## Phone, focus, announcement

- **Phone at 390×844:** question 1 starts at about 610 px, on the first screen of the result. No
  horizontal overflow at 390 or 1440, light or dark.
- **Focus:** inputs use `focus:ring-2 focus:ring-focus`. Links use `TextAction`.
- **Numbering:** step numbers are `aria-hidden`, because the `<ol>` already numbers the steps.
- **Announcement:** the existing live region says "Result below. No map matches it, rather than a
  wrong one. Then three questions to read it yourself." It adds "The closest maps are listed." when
  there are closest maps.
