# Deep citation audit, batch 04 (2026-11-10)

Maps: `us-iran-conflict`, `minneapolis-shooting`, `immigration-national-identity`.

Method: every `sourceUrl` was loaded in this session (curl, PDF text extracted with `pdftotext`) and each number, name, date and quotation in the card was compared with the page. Where the publisher refused the fetch (403 or a bot wall), the page was read through a web.archive.org snapshot, or, for journal DOIs, through the Crossref-deposited abstract. Wikipedia was used only to corroborate a quotation or count when the primary text could not be read; it is never cited. No URL was written that was not loaded.

Totals: 48 cards checked, 27 corrected, 2 replaced, 19 unchanged. The three `keystone_fact` sources were checked too and all three checked out.
