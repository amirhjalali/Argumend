# Product critique synthesis — four-model round (2026-08-11)

Trigger: founder verdict — "so many issues, it does not look compelling; not good enough to share with anyone." Critics: codex (gpt-5.5, information architecture), grok (shareability/voice), opencode (first-time-reader confusion), kimi (insight density). Gemini CLI unavailable (individual tier sunset). Raw critiques in this directory. All four were given the rendered flagship page, homepage, and Explore index — not the code.

## The convergent verdict

All four independently: **first-screen bounce.** The research spine has edges; the experience sandblasts them into "beige corpus UI" (grok). "The reader learns a lot about Argumend's scrupulousness and little about AI and jobs" (kimi). The page is front-loaded with apparatus and back-loaded with payoff — exactly backwards for a phone visitor.

Ten converged findings (4/4 or 3/4 agreement):

1. **Inventory instead of insight** — "4 positions · 54 claims · 50 pieces of evidence" advertises workload, teaches nothing. Kill; lead with the sharpest thing the map discovered.
2. **Position cards are seminar essays** — 36-word nominalized sentences, jargon labels ("Automation-panic redux"). Human label + one vivid line; steelman behind expand.
3. **Cruxes — the actual product — read like database rows** terminated by tag soup ("Empirical Contested Hidden assumption Thin evidence"). Rewrite as question headlines; demote tags.
4. **The killer facts are buried ~200 lines deep**: 16%-decline vs 4%-unemployment paradox, Klarna 700-agents reversal, women 2.7×, $34.9K care-work escape valve. Surface as steal-able cards.
5. **Trust apparatus swamps content** — interest/scope/status-basis notes at full weight everywhere; methodology footer reads like a hallucination disclaimer. Demote to badges/one line; disclosure stays, word count drops.
6. **"Explore the full argument" is a 45-row homework dump.** Collapse behind researcher mode.
7. **No people, no stakes** — a mass-unemployment page nobody is *in*. Add the identity hook ("if you're 22–25...").
8. **No payoff/ending** — add "what you can honestly say after five minutes."
9. **Funnel sells metrics, not findings** — Balance/Weight chrome on every card; featured copy sells process ("inspectable"). *(Card chrome on legacy surfaces deferred — separate change.)*
10. **The definitional trap is the frame, not item 4** — "three fights in a trench coat" belongs in the TL;DR.

## What was implemented (same day)

- Header: stats bar removed → identity hook + "three fights in a trench coat" TL;DR card.
- Positions: humanized labels ("Yes — it's already starting" / "No — we've panicked before" / "Not fewer jobs — worse ones" / "Wrong question — who decides?"), one-line summaries, full steelman + constituency behind expand.
- Four "numbers to steal" insight cards (−16%, 700 'agents', 2.7×, $34,900).
- Cruxes retitled "The whole fight turns on five questions"; question-form headlines from `summary`; rubric renamed (Why people fight about it / What would settle it / So what); tags reduced to the two that matter.
- Full-claims list collapsed behind "Researcher mode"; takeaways block added; methodology footer reduced to one line linking /methodology.
- Featured card on /topics sells the tension, not the apparatus.
- Deferred (recorded, not dropped): Balance/Weight chrome on legacy homepage/Explore cards; homepage hero copy; per-crux share cards.

## The strategic lesson (for every future topic)

Auditability is the platform's spine, but it is **inspection depth, not surface texture**. Default view = the argument at full human voltage; the scrupulousness one tap away. "Same integrity, a fifth of the words" (kimi).
