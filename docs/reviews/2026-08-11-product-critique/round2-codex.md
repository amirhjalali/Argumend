   **Why it fails:** The BLS projection supports “home-health care is the largest projected job-growth occupation,” not that displaced office workers will land there by default. This is a provocative inference presented as fact.  
   **Concrete fix:** Rewrite as: “the kind of low-paid work the economy is actually adding fastest.” Then make the value question explicit: “If an $80K office track becomes a $35K care job, did the economy adjust?”
