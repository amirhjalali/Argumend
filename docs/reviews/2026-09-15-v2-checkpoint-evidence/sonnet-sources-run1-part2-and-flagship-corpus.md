## Systematic issues (ranked by count across 23 reports)

1. **Primary crux is not the contested load-bearing claim — 12/23.** The engine's top pick is an undisputed premise (27 six-riders, 28 backstop, 32 forty-minutes, 33 outside-hour, us-israel death-toll), the position or the question restated (24, 26, 29, 36), the parties' *explicit common ground* (35), a generalization minted from a quip (40), or a one-position "the real question is…" reframing (ai-mass-unemployment, us-israel). In 7 of those (28, 32, 33, 35, 36, 40, us-israel) the crux a careful human would name is present but ranked 2nd or 3rd. This is the single biggest quality problem and it lands in the share headline ("The argument turns on: …").
   Files: 24, 26, 27, 28, 29, 32, 33, 35, 36, 40, corpus/ai-mass-unemployment, corpus/us-israel-support.

2. **Diagnosis pattern contradicts the report's own contents — 10/20 authored + 1 corpus.**
   - `mostly-common-ground` fires with `sharedGround=low` (21, 23, 32) or on a trust dispute (38): 4 files; headline "They agree more than the argument makes it seem" is false in 3 of them. Looks like a count-based rule (commonGround ≥ disagreements) rather than the spec's "high common ground and at most one narrow disagreement".
   - `mixed-disagreement` as default — 8/20 (24, 26, 29, 33, 34, 35, 36, 40); only 33 is genuinely mixed. 35 and 36 are textbook single-empirical-crux inputs; **`single-empirical-crux` fired 0 times in 20 files.** 29 is definitional, 34 is value-conflict; 26/40 are the documented procedural fallback but the headline "several disagreements stacked together" misdescribes them.
   - `primaryType` / pattern disagrees with the primary crux's own type: 31 (predictive vs normative), 30 (value vs evidence-resolvable), 35 (empirical vs procedural), 40 (empirical vs trust), ai-mass (causal vs priority).
   Files: 21, 23, 29, 30, 31, 32, 34, 35, 36, 38, 40, corpus/ai-mass-unemployment.

3. **"Minted fallback stake for primary crux claim" shown in the accountability box — 11/23.** `participantId=None`, `role=unclear`, `targetConclusion` = the report's question string, consequence "The source does not state what changes if this claim is false." Junk in a user-facing box; it also correlates with issue 1 (it appears exactly when the primary crux claim has no owner-stake, i.e. when the crux is not a participant's contested reason).
