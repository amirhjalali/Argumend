# Gemini Instructions

Read `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` in this repo before changing anything. They hold the commands, conventions, design system and feature flags. The copy rules below apply to every new or edited string (UI, map data, blog, docs, alt text, commit messages).

- No em dashes (U+2014). Reword with a comma, colon, period or parentheses; do not substitute " - ", "--" or an en dash. `lib/copyStyle.test.ts` fails if any file gains one.
- Never use amber or tangerine colors.
- Argumend never names a winner, and copy must not imply one.
- Do not claim "every map" follows a process unless that is true of every map.
- Every figure needs a linked source that states it.
